[h1]Description[/h1]
[b]Cargo Containers Expanded[/b] is a maintained RimWorld 1.6 fork of AOBA's Cargo Container mod. It stores large resource payloads in compact buildings and lets you extract the contents later through bills.

[b]This fork replaces the original mod. Do not enable both together.[/b]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Stuffable containers:[/b] Full and half containers for metallic, woody, stony, fabric, and leathery resources.
[*][b]Fixed payload storage:[/b] Containers and tanks for survival meals, medicine, drugs, beer, neutroamine, wort, and chemfuel.
[*][b]Extraction bills:[/b] Extract batches of 1, 25, or 100, including exact final partial batches.
[*][b]Safe deconstruction:[/b] Return remaining payload and frame materials without duplicate refunds or lost contents.
[*][b]Refrigeration:[/b] Powered refrigerated containers pause rot. Unpowered payload takes twice as long to rot as the same loose item.
[*][b]Payload-aware wealth:[/b] Stored contents contribute 10% of their loose-item market value, decreasing as items are extracted.
[*][b]Reduced runtime overhead:[/b] Container checks, inert tank ticking, and recipe filtering avoid unnecessary repeated work.
[/list]

[h1]How to Use[/h1]
Build a resource container using the material you want it to hold, or choose a fixed-payload container or tank. Select the completed container and add an extraction bill for 1, 25, or 100 items. A final bill automatically returns the exact amount remaining.

Refrigerated containers use a rottable resource as their construction material and payload. Keep them powered to pause rot. Without power, the payload continues aging at twice the loose item's normal rot duration.

[list]

[*][b]Save compatibility:[/b] Existing def names and save keys are preserved for the supported RimWorld 1.6 fork data.
[*][b]Localization:[/b] English is complete. Legacy Simplified and Traditional Chinese definitions remain translated; newer extraction and refrigeration text falls back to English.
[*][b]Balance:[/b] The 10% payload wealth value and 2x unpowered rot duration are intentional.
[/list]

[h1]Fork History[/h1]
AOBA created the original Cargo Container mod. This fork keeps its compact-storage idea available on RimWorld 1.6 while adding payload extraction, exact final batches, safe deconstruction refunds, refrigerated containers, payload-aware market value, modern packaging, and runtime safety improvements.
